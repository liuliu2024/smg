# External integration and production prerequisites

P0 is local demonstration only. No real payments, student records or external notifications are used.

Before production obtain and validate school SSO protocol and credentials, WeCom identity mapping and permissions, library protocol, authorized payment entity, printer device protocol, malware scanning, backup and restore, monitoring, retention policy, capacity testing and school approval.

Production must reject demo authentication. Manual fulfillment is not a printer device receipt. Simulated payments are not revenue. SQLite demonstration results do not establish capacity for 5,000 users.

No public deployment is authorized or performed.
